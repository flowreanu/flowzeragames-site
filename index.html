FAQ

Questions?

Do I need TikFinity or any paid tool?

No. MIND RUSH includes a small connector that reads your LIVE chat, likes, follows and gifts directly from your username. Nothing else to buy or subscribe to.

Does it work on a phone-only stream?

No. The game runs on a Windows PC and is shown on stream with Window Capture in TikTok LIVE Studio or OBS. Your viewers can watch and play from any phone.

Is the leaderboard saved between streams?

Yes. Points, ranks and weekly champions are saved on your PC, with automatic backups, so your community keeps climbing every LIVE.

What do viewers need to do?

Just comment A, B, C or D. Shop commands like !x2 or !5050 are optional extras for regulars who've saved up points.

Can I use my webcam with it?

Yes. There's a built-in camera circle next to the logo. You can use your webcam, a green-screen hole, or turn it off.

Is this made by TikTok?

No. FlowZeraGames is an independent creator and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok. Follow TikTok's LIVE guidelines when you stream.
