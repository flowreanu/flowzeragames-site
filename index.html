FAQ

Questions?

How does the free trial work?

Click any "Start trial" button and check out on Whop. You get the download and your personal license key right away and can play for 7 days for free. After that it's $9.99 a month per game (URSITOAREA is $13 a month with no trial, ALL-ACCESS is $20 a month for every game). Cancel anytime from your Whop account. The key stops working at the end of the period you paid for.

Why do I need a license key?

The key unlocks the game files and links the game to your TikTok account. It's how we keep the games fair for the creators who pay for them. The game checks the key when it starts and every few minutes. If your internet drops, it keeps running for 30 minutes.

Can I use one key on two TikTok accounts?

No. One key works for one creator and one TikTok account. If you change your account, contact us and we'll move it.

Do I need TikFinity or any paid tool?

No. Each game includes a small connector that reads your LIVE chat, likes, follows and gifts directly from your username. You don't need to buy anything else.

Does it work on a phone-only stream?

No. The games run on a Windows PC and are shown on stream with Window Capture in TikTok LIVE Studio or OBS. Your viewers can watch and play from any phone.

Is the leaderboard saved between streams?

Yes. Points, ranks and weekly champions are saved on your PC, with automatic backups, so your community keeps climbing every LIVE.

What's new in MIND RUSH 2.0?

A new layout with the questions at the top, the Boss Question, Mystery Words with letters that appear by themselves, the Final Duel, Shield and Freeze commands, a category vote by typing (no gifts), and a x4 limit on the multiplier so scores stay fair.

Should I show my camera?

Yes, we strongly recommend it. TikTok can restrict LIVEs that only show a game with no creator. Turn your camera on, talk to your viewers and react to the game. MIND RUSH has a built-in camera circle.

Is this made by TikTok?

No. FlowZeraGames is an independent creator and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok. Follow TikTok's LIVE guidelines when you stream.
